Behavior notes

No network, ever. The comparison is against your repo's last-fetched picture of the remote. If you fetch mid-review, the existing "Diff out of date · Refresh" banner appears (the staleness fingerprint includes the remote-tracking tip) and one click recomputes.
Everything else just works. Staging files from the diff, review snapshots, Ask AI context, and feedback exports all treat it like any other mode; both server runtimes picked it up through the shared modules with zero server edits.
The one sharp edge: there is no merge-base. If the remote has commits you haven't pulled, those render as reversed hunks — a teammate's additions appear as your deletions. Literally correct for "local vs remote", but different from what a PR view would show.
