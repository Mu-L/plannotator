Loading strategy — why on-demand, not optimistic

No preloading, and that is a decision, not a shortcut. Every resolve is a real ripgrep process spawn against the working tree. Optimistically warming visible tokens would spawn dozens of subprocesses per scroll for cards nobody opens — the exact pathology the opencode-serve leak taught this codebase to never build. Instead: dwell gating spawns at most one rg per 350 ms of genuine attention, single-in-flight with abort, an identifier/keyword stoplist filters non-symbols before any request, and the LRU makes repeat attention free. One accepted residual, bounded and documented: the server cannot kill an rg the client aborted — worst case one orphaned process per dwell, running to its 3 s cap; single-flight coalescing is the named escalation if real use ever shows pile-ups, deliberately not built now.
